# Why We Migrated Our Build Pipeline

In today's fast-paced development landscape, it's important to note that build reliability plays a crucial role in shipping quality software. Our team recently embarked on a journey to overhaul our comprehensive CI setup, and the results were transformative.

Here's the thing: our old pipeline wasn't just slow, it was fundamentally broken. The problem? Nobody trusted it. Studies show that developers ignore flaky tests, and our team was no exception.

We decided to leverage a more robust solution. We evaluated the landscape. We compared the options. We chose GitHub Actions.

The migration was seamless. Despite its challenges, the new pipeline has been a game-changer for our workflow, underscoring the importance of investing in developer tooling. Experts say that teams who prioritize CI see significant productivity gains.

It's not just about speed, it's about trust. Our builds now run in eight minutes, delivering actionable insights to every engineer on the team.

## Conclusion

In summary, migrating our build pipeline was crucial, vital work that empowered our team to move faster. At the end of the day, it's about shipping with confidence.
